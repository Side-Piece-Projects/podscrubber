Podcasts, minus the ads.

PodScrubber is a desktop app for Windows that downloads podcast episodes, finds the ads in them, and lets you trim them out before you listen.

  • Finds ads for youTranscribes each episode on your computer and flags sponsor reads and inserted ads, with the reasons it thinks so.
  • Remembers ads it has seenRecognises the same recorded ad when it turns up in another episode.
  • You stay in controlReview every suggestion on a waveform with the transcript beside it, and adjust the cuts yourself.
  • Exports anywhereSave trimmed episodes as files, or send them to your own Google Drive to listen on your other devices.

Runs on your computer

Downloading, transcription and editing all happen on your own machine. PodScrubber has no account to sign up for and no servers of its own; it doesn't collect information about you or what you listen to. If you choose to connect Google Drive, trimmed episodes go straight from your computer to your own Drive. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app touches.

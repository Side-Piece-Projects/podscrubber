Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

In short: PodScrubber runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no accounts, and it doesn't collect, sell or share information about you. If you connect Google Drive, it can only see and change the files it creates there itself.

What stays on your computer

Everything PodScrubber works with is stored in its own folder on your computer: the shows you follow, downloaded episodes, transcripts it makes, the ads you mark, its library of recognised ads, and your settings. Transcription and ad detection run on your computer; your audio and transcripts are never uploaded anywhere by PodScrubber, except to your own Google Drive if you choose to export there.

What PodScrubber connects to

To do its job, the app connects directly from your computer to:

  • Podcast publishers and their hosting services, to read show feeds and download episodes, as any podcast app does.
  • Apple's public podcast directory, when you search for a show to add.
  • GitHub and Hugging Face, once, to download the speech-recognition engine and model used for ad detection.
  • Google, only if you connect Google Drive (see below).

Like any website you visit, these services can see your computer's internet address when it connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. PodScrubber sends them nothing beyond what's needed for the request.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you do:

  • You sign in with Google in your web browser and approve access. PodScrubber asks only for the “See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app” permission (drive.file).
  • With it, PodScrubber creates a PodScrubber folder in your Drive and uploads the episodes you export into it. It can only see and change files and folders it created itself. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.
  • PodScrubber reads your Drive account's name and email address only to show you which account is connected.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on your computer, in Windows Credential Manager. It is never sent anywhere except to Google, to keep you signed in.
  • Information received from Google is used only to provide these features. It isn't stored anywhere other than your computer, isn't shared with anyone, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to train AI or machine-learning models.

PodScrubber's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, use Disconnect Google Drive in PodScrubber, which also cancels the app's access with Google. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Files PodScrubber already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.

What PodScrubber doesn't do

  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No advertising.
  • No accounts, and no servers that receive your data.
  • No selling or sharing of any information about you.

Deleting your data

Removing a show or a downloaded episode in PodScrubber deletes its files from your computer. Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder (%APPDATA%\com.podscrubber.app) removes everything it stored. Files in your Google Drive are yours to keep or delete.

Children

PodScrubber isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
